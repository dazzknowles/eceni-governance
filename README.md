# eceni-governance

The canonical source of governance for Eceni product and engineering work.

The current Eceni Governance baseline is **1.3.0**, adopted on 26 September 2026.

## Documents

- [Eceni Commandments](COMMANDMENTS.md) — binding portfolio-level principles for product and engineering judgement.
- [Eceni Laws](LAWS.md) — concrete engineering rules, applicability, exceptions and evidence requirements.
- [Engineering culture](ENGINEERING-CULTURE.md) — broader aspirations and ways of working for humans and AI collaborators.
- [Checks](CHECKS.md) — objective automated enforcement, including implementation status.
- [C# implementation guide](guides/CSHARP.md)
- [MariaDB implementation guide](guides/MARIADB.md)

Culture is general and aspirational. The Commandments make its essential boundaries binding. Laws will translate those boundaries into concrete rules and defaults; Checks will enforce Laws only where automation is reliable and proportionate.

## Governance design principle

> **High engineering standards, low administrative friction.**

Eceni Governance defines demanding engineering outcomes while keeping the machinery used to demonstrate them proportionate. Automate objective checks once; use judgement where automation cannot decide reliably; scale evidence and review to consequence; and do not create documents or approval ceremonies that add no useful authority, learning or protection.

Lightweight Governance does not mean lower-quality engineering. It means maximum autonomy inside explicit authority, with recurring friction removed and consequential departures made visible without burdening routine compliant work.

**Introduced in Governance baseline:** 1.3.0
