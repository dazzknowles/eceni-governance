# eceni-governance

The canonical source of governance for Eceni product and engineering work.

The current Eceni Governance baseline is **1.2.0**, adopted on 24 September 2026.

## Documents

- [Eceni Commandments](COMMANDMENTS.md) — binding portfolio-level principles for product and engineering judgement.
- [Eceni Laws](LAWS.md) — concrete engineering rules, applicability, exceptions and evidence requirements.
- [Engineering culture](ENGINEERING-CULTURE.md) — broader aspirations and ways of working for humans and AI collaborators.
- [Checks](CHECKS.md) — objective automated enforcement, including implementation status.
- [C# implementation guide](guides/CSHARP.md)
- [MariaDB implementation guide](guides/MARIADB.md)

Culture is general and aspirational. The Commandments make its essential boundaries binding. Laws will translate those boundaries into concrete rules and defaults; Checks will enforce Laws only where automation is reliable and proportionate.

## Baseline history

- **1.0.0 — 23 September 2026:** Engineering Culture and Eceni Commandments.
- **1.1.0 — 24 September 2026:** Initial Laws, Check catalogue, and C# and MariaDB implementation guides.
- **1.2.0 — 24 September 2026:** Technology foundations require explicit authority where no adopted project or portfolio default exists.
